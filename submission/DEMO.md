## What the video should show

1. The problem: India's cultural heritage is vast, and most people meet it only
   through text they have to search for and read.
2. The device: ESP32 + INMP441, idle, sending no audio.
3. Saying "Nakshatra": the on-device model fires and the website switches to
   "Nakshatra detected", then "Listening".
4. A question ("Why do we celebrate Diwali?") appearing word by word as it is
   recognised, then the spoken answer.
5. Two or three more questions from different themes (a monument, a dance, a craft).
6. A confusable word ("Natak", "Lakshan") that does *not* wake the device.
7. The Explore, Featured and Map sections, and the latency read-out.

The step-by-step script is in the README, section "SIH demonstration instructions".
